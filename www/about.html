Personal Introduction

Hey, I'm Joshua — a college student who genuinely enjoys the moment an idea on paper turns into something you can actually tap and use on a screen. I got into IT because I liked taking things apart to see how they worked, and mobile development scratched that itch better than anything else: one app touches design, logic, and real people's daily habits all at once.

Outside of coursework, I spend a lot of time experimenting with small side projects, tinkering with UI ideas, and occasionally falling down a rabbit hole researching something completely unrelated just because it's interesting. I like environments where curiosity is actually useful, not just tolerated.
